1. Purpose

   This procedure defines the process for identifying hazards, estimating and
   evaluating risk, controlling risk, and monitoring the effectiveness of risk
   controls across the product lifecycle.

2. Scope

   Applies to all medical devices, accessories and software developed,
   manufactured or distributed by the organization, from concept through
   post-production.

3. References

   Corrective and Preventive Action Procedure. Complaint Handling Procedure.
   Design Control Procedure. Change Control Procedure. Post-Market Surveillance
   Procedure.

4. Definitions

   Hazard — a potential source of harm. Harm — injury or damage to health.
   Risk — the combination of the probability of occurrence of harm and the
   severity of that harm. Residual risk — the risk remaining after risk control
   measures have been implemented.

5. Risk Management Planning

   5.1 A risk management plan shall be established for each device before design
       activity begins, and shall be approved by the Head of Risk Management.

   5.2 The plan shall define the criteria for risk acceptability, and those
       criteria shall be established before any risk is evaluated against them.

   5.3 The plan shall identify the scope of the activities covered, the
       lifecycle phases to which they apply, the personnel responsible, and the
       method by which production and post-production information will be
       reviewed.

   5.4 Any change to the risk management plan during the project shall be
       recorded with its rationale and the date of the change.

6. Hazard Identification

   6.1 Known and foreseeable hazards shall be identified for both normal use and
       fault conditions, and the identification shall cover reasonably
       foreseeable misuse.

   6.2 The record shall document the method used to identify hazards and the
       inputs relied upon, including relevant post-market and complaint data for
       predicate or predecessor products.

   6.3 For each hazard, the foreseeable sequence of events leading to a
       hazardous situation and to harm shall be documented.

7. Risk Estimation

   7.1 For each hazardous situation, the severity of harm and the probability of
       its occurrence shall be estimated using the scales defined in the risk
       management plan.

   7.2 The basis for each probability estimate shall be recorded, and where
       quantitative data is unavailable the record shall state that a
       qualitative estimate was used and why.

   7.3 Severity and probability ratings shall be applied consistently across the
       risk management file, and a rating for a given hazardous situation shall
       not differ between documents without a recorded justification.

8. Risk Evaluation

   8.1 Each estimated risk shall be evaluated against the acceptability criteria
       defined in the risk management plan, and the evaluation result shall be
       recorded.

   8.2 The acceptability threshold applied shall be the one stated in the
       approved plan, and any deviation shall be documented and approved before
       it is applied.

9. Risk Control

   9.1 Risk control measures shall be selected in the following order of
       priority: first, inherently safe design and manufacture; second,
       protective measures in the device itself or in manufacture; third,
       information for safety such as labeling and training.

   9.2 Where a lower-priority control is selected, the record shall document why
       the higher-priority options were not practicable.

   9.3 Information for safety shall not be recorded as the sole control for a
       risk whose severity is rated at the highest tier defined in the plan
       without a documented justification approved by the Head of Risk
       Management.

   9.4 The implementation of each risk control measure shall be verified, and
       its effectiveness in reducing the risk shall be verified separately from
       its implementation.

   9.5 Each risk control measure shall be assessed for whether it introduces new
       hazards or increases an existing risk, and any such effect shall be fed
       back into Sections 6 through 8.

10. Residual Risk

    10.1 The residual risk remaining after each risk control measure shall be
         estimated and evaluated against the criteria in the plan.

    10.2 Where a residual risk is judged not acceptable and further control is
         not practicable, a benefit-risk analysis shall be performed and
         recorded.

    10.3 Residual risks that remain shall be disclosed in the accompanying
         documentation where a user or patient needs that information to use the
         device safely.

11. Overall Residual Risk

    11.1 After all risk control measures have been implemented and verified, the
         overall residual risk of the device shall be evaluated against the
         criteria in the plan, considered as a whole rather than risk by risk.

    11.2 The overall residual risk evaluation shall be recorded as a distinct
         conclusion with its own justification, and shall not be inferred from
         the acceptability of the individual risks.

12. Production and Post-Production Information

    12.1 A system shall be maintained to collect and review information about
         the device in production and in the field, including complaints,
         service data, nonconformities and publicly reported events for similar
         devices.

    12.2 Collected information shall be assessed for whether it affects a
         previously estimated probability, severity, or acceptability
         conclusion, and the assessment shall be recorded.

    12.3 Where the collected information shows a risk is no longer acceptable,
         the impact on implemented risk controls shall be evaluated and a
         corrective action record shall be opened.

13. Risk Management File

    13.1 A risk management file shall be maintained for each device, providing
         traceability from each identified hazard to its risk analysis, risk
         evaluation, risk control measures and verification of effectiveness.

    13.2 A risk management report shall be produced before commercial release,
         and shall record that the plan was implemented as written, that the
         overall residual risk is acceptable, and that the means of collecting
         production and post-production information are in place.

    13.3 The report shall be reviewed and approved by the Head of Risk
         Management, and the approval date shall not precede the date of the
         evidence relied upon.

14. Records

    All records generated under this procedure shall be retained for the
    lifetime of the device plus the retention period defined in the records
    control procedure, and shall be traceable to the device configuration to
    which they apply.
