# Quality System Procedure — Risk Management

**Document No.:** QSP-0021
**Rev:** 4
**Effective Date:** 2025-02-01
**Owner:** Director of Systems Engineering

---

1. Purpose

   This procedure defines the risk management framework applied to Northlake
   devices across the product lifecycle, consistent with an ISO 14971 framework.

2. Scope

   Applies to all Northlake devices and to the risk management files and design
   FMEAs derived under this procedure.

3. References

   QSP-0008 Complaint Handling. QSP-0012 Corrective and Preventive Action.
   QSP-0015 Engineering Change Control.

4. Risk Management Planning

   4.1 A risk management plan shall be established for each device before design
       activity begins, and shall define the risk acceptability criteria before
       any risk is evaluated against them.

5. Risk Estimation and Evaluation

   5.1 Severity and probability ratings shall be applied consistently across the
       risk management file and the design FMEA, and a rating for a given hazard
       shall not differ between documents without a recorded justification.

   5.2 Each estimated risk shall be evaluated against the acceptability threshold
       stated in the approved plan.

6. Risk Control

   6.1 Risk control measures shall be selected in the priority order of inherently
       safe design, then protective measures, then information for safety, and the
       record shall justify any use of a lower-priority control.

   6.2 The implementation and the effectiveness of each risk control shall be
       verified, and each control shall be assessed for whether it introduces a
       new hazard.

7. Overall Residual Risk

   7.1 The overall residual risk shall be evaluated as a whole and recorded as a
       distinct conclusion. Any individual residual risk above the acceptability
       threshold requires a documented benefit-risk analysis before the overall
       residual risk is judged acceptable.

8. Production and Post-Production

   8.1 Complaint, service, and field information shall be reviewed against the
       risk estimates, and the risk file shall be updated where that information
       shows an estimate is no longer valid.

   8.2 The risk management review authorizing commercial release shall be recorded,
       and its date shall not precede the evidence it relies upon or the release
       it authorizes.
