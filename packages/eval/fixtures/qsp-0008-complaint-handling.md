# Quality System Procedure — Complaint Handling and MDR Reportability

**Document No.:** QSP-0008
**Rev:** 5
**Effective Date:** 2025-03-15
**Owner:** Director of Quality Assurance

---

1. Purpose

   This procedure defines the process for receiving, evaluating, investigating,
   and closing complaints, and for determining medical device reporting (MDR)
   obligations.

2. Scope

   Applies to all complaints received about Northlake devices from any source.

3. References

   QSP-0012 Corrective and Preventive Action. QSP-0021 Risk Management. 21 CFR
   Part 803 Medical Device Reporting.

4. Definitions

   Complaint — any written, electronic, or oral communication alleging
   deficiencies related to the identity, quality, durability, reliability,
   safety, effectiveness, or performance of a device after it is released for
   distribution.

5. Complaint Intake and Evaluation

   5.1 Every complaint shall be recorded with the date received, the reporter,
       the affected device and, where known, the lot or serial and software
       version.

   5.2 Every complaint shall be evaluated for whether it represents an event that
       must be reported under 21 CFR Part 803, and that reportability evaluation
       shall be documented in the complaint record.

   5.3 Where a complaint describes an event that may have caused or contributed to
       a death or serious injury, the record shall be escalated to the Medical
       Safety Officer for a reportability determination, and the determination and
       its basis shall be recorded before the complaint is closed.

6. Investigation

   6.1 Each complaint shall be investigated to a depth proportionate to the risk,
       and the investigation and its conclusion shall be recorded.

   6.2 Where a complaint indicates a potential systemic problem, a CAPA shall be
       opened under QSP-0012 and referenced in the complaint record.

7. Linkage to Risk

   7.1 Complaint information shall be reviewed against the risk management file
       for the device, and where a complaint indicates that a risk estimate is no
       longer valid, the risk file shall be updated under QSP-0021.

8. Closure

   8.1 A complaint shall not be closed until its investigation, its reportability
       determination, and any linked CAPA reference are recorded.
