# Quality System Procedure — Production and Process Controls (Good Manufacturing Practice)

**Document No.:** QSP-0025
**Rev:** 2
**Effective Date:** 2025-04-01
**Owner:** Director of Manufacturing

---

1. Purpose

   This procedure defines the controls governing the manufacture of Northlake
   devices so that finished product conforms to its specifications. It
   establishes the good-manufacturing-practice requirements for production
   processes, their validation, in-process controls, and the device history
   record.

2. Scope

   Applies to all production and process steps for released Northlake devices,
   including the VP-400 and SP-200 platforms and the shared PCA-4400 pump-control
   board, and to any process whose output cannot be fully verified by subsequent
   inspection or test.

3. References

   QSP-0015 Engineering Change Control. QSP-0021 Risk Management. QSP-0012
   Corrective and Preventive Action. 21 CFR Part 820 Subpart G (Production and
   Process Controls) and Subpart H (Acceptance Activities).

4. Definitions

   Process validation — establishing by objective evidence that a process
   consistently produces a result meeting its predetermined specifications. DHR —
   Device History Record. In-process control — a control applied during
   production rather than on the finished device.

5. Production Process Control

   5.1 Each production process that can affect device quality shall be carried
       out under a controlled, documented procedure that defines the process
       parameters, the equipment, and the acceptance criteria.

   5.2 Where process parameters must be monitored and controlled to ensure
       conformance, the parameters shall be identified, monitored, and recorded,
       and the record shall be retained in the device history record.

   5.3 Where the results of a process cannot be fully verified by subsequent
       inspection or test, the process shall be validated with a documented
       procedure, and the validation shall be approved before the process is
       used for production.

6. Process Validation and Revalidation

   6.1 A process validation shall define the parameters to be controlled, their
       operating ranges, the number of production runs evaluated, and the
       acceptance criteria, and shall record the qualified personnel who
       approved it.

   6.2 Where a validated process is changed, or where the process or product
       specification changes, the change shall be assessed for its effect on the
       validated state under QSP-0015, and the process shall be revalidated to
       the extent the assessment determines is necessary.

   6.3 Where a validated process is controlled by software, that software shall
       be validated for its intended use, and the validation shall be recorded.

7. Equipment, Calibration, and Environment

   7.1 Equipment used in production shall be maintained on a defined schedule,
       and each maintenance activity shall be recorded.

   7.2 Measuring and test equipment used to demonstrate conformance shall be
       calibrated against a traceable standard at a defined interval, and
       equipment found out of calibration shall trigger an assessment of the
       product accepted on its readings since the last valid calibration.

   7.3 Where environmental conditions could reasonably affect product quality,
       those conditions shall be controlled, monitored, and recorded.

8. In-Process Control and Acceptance

   8.1 In-process product shall be controlled so that it is inspected or tested
       at the points defined in the process procedure, and product shall not
       proceed to the next step until the required in-process acceptance is
       recorded.

   8.2 Finished product shall not be released for distribution until the
       activities required by the device master record are completed and the
       associated records are reviewed and approved.

   8.3 The acceptance status of product shall be identified throughout
       production so that only product that has passed the required acceptance
       activities is used or distributed.

9. Nonconforming Product

   9.1 Product that does not conform to specification shall be identified,
       segregated where practicable, and dispositioned under a documented
       procedure, and the disposition and its justification shall be recorded.

   9.2 Where nonconforming product is reworked, the rework shall be performed
       under a procedure that has been assessed for any adverse effect on the
       product, and the reworked product shall be re-inspected or re-tested.

10. Device History Record and Traceability

    10.1 A device history record shall be maintained for each batch, lot, or
         unit that demonstrates the device was manufactured in accordance with
         the device master record, including the acceptance records and the
         primary identification and control number of the unit.

    10.2 Where a component or process is shared across more than one device
         model, the records shall permit tracing an affected component or
         process to every device model it was used in.

11. Personnel and Training

    11.1 Personnel performing a controlled production or inspection activity
         shall be trained for that activity, and the training shall be recorded
         before the activity is performed unsupervised.

12. Records

    All records generated under this procedure shall be retained for the period
    defined in the records control procedure and shall be traceable to the
    device configuration and production lot to which they apply.
