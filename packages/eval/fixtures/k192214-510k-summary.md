# 510(k) Summary — VP-400 Volumetric Infusion Pump

**510(k) Number:** K192214
**Applicant:** Northlake Medical Systems, Inc.
**Date Prepared:** 2019-06-14
**Device:** VP-400 Volumetric Infusion Pump
**Classification:** Class II, 21 CFR 880.5725, product code FRN
**Predicate Device:** Northlake VP-300 Volumetric Infusion Pump (K151882)

---

## 1. Device Description

The VP-400 is a microprocessor-controlled large-volume volumetric infusion pump
intended for the controlled delivery of intravenous fluids and medications to
adult and pediatric patients in professional healthcare facilities. Delivery is
by a linear peristaltic mechanism acting on a disposable administration set
cassette.

The device runs application software version **3.1.4** on the pump controller
(board PCA-4400) and an independent safety-controller firmware, version **4.1**,
on a separate microcontroller. The safety controller provides independent
watchdog monitoring, air-in-line detection, and downstream occlusion detection.

## 2. Intended Use / Indications for Use

The VP-400 is indicated for the delivery of fluids requiring controlled infusion,
including medications, blood and blood products, and parenteral nutrition, by
clinically trained personnel in a hospital or clinical setting.

## 3. Technological Characteristics

The VP-400 shares the pumping mechanism, user interface architecture, and safety
subsystem of the predicate VP-300. Differences from the predicate are the updated
pump-control board (PCA-4400), a revised display, and the current software and
firmware versions.

Downstream occlusion is detected by a pressure transducer in the fluid path. The
safety controller declares an occlusion when the measured downstream pressure
exceeds the configured occlusion pressure threshold, nominally **525 mmHg** at the
default sensitivity setting, for longer than the debounce interval. The occlusion
pressure threshold and alarm behavior are established characteristics of the
cleared device and were evaluated in the performance testing below.

## 4. Performance Data

Bench performance testing was conducted to the applicable clauses of IEC 60601-2-24
(particular requirements for infusion pumps). Occlusion-detection performance,
including alarm pressure and time-to-alarm at the nominal threshold, was tested and
met the acceptance criteria. Delivery accuracy was demonstrated at ±5% across the
labeled flow-rate range. Electrical safety and electromagnetic compatibility were
evaluated to IEC 60601-1 and IEC 60601-1-2. Software was documented at a
Major level of concern.

## 5. Substantial Equivalence

The VP-400 has the same intended use as the predicate VP-300 and the same
fundamental scientific technology. The occlusion-detection subsystem, delivery
mechanism, and safety architecture are equivalent to the predicate. The
differences do not raise new questions of safety or effectiveness. Northlake
concludes that the VP-400 is substantially equivalent to the predicate device.

## 6. Conclusion

Based on the intended use, technological characteristics, and performance data,
the VP-400 Volumetric Infusion Pump is substantially equivalent to the legally
marketed predicate device K151882.
