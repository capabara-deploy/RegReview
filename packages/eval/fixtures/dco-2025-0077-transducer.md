# Engineering Change Order — Pressure Transducer Part Substitution (PCA-4400)

**Record No.:** DCO-2025-0077
**Rev:** 2
**Date:** 2025-10-08
**Originator:** K. Sethi, Design Engineering
**Governing procedure:** QSP-0015 Engineering Change Control and Change Assessment

---

## 1. Reason for Change

The pressure transducer used on the PCA-4400 pump-control board is being
discontinued by the supplier. This change substitutes an alternate transducer of
the same nominal specification.

## 2. Affected Models

The PCA-4400 board is used in both the **VP-400** volumetric infusion pump and the
**SP-200** syringe pump. Both models are affected by this change.

## 3. Affected Documents

- DOS-0400 (VP-400 Design Output Specification / DMR index) — bill of materials
  updated.

## 4. Description of Change

The existing pressure transducer is replaced with an alternate part. The alternate
was characterized on the bench and found to have equivalent pressure-vs-output
response within the specified tolerance band.

## 5. Change Assessment

The substitution is a like-for-like component change characterized as equivalent.
The submission determination is recorded under the change-control process.

## 6. Risk File Assessment

The change was assessed against the VP-400 risk management file. No change to the
VP-400 risk estimates was identified.
