# Risk Management Plan — VP-400 Volumetric Infusion Pump

**Document No.:** RMP-VP400
**Rev:** 2
**Date:** 2025-07-10
**Prepared by:** L. Marchetti, Systems Engineering
**Approved by:** S. Okafor, Quality Manager
**Applicable standard:** ISO 14971:2019

---

## 1. Scope

This plan governs the risk management activities for the VP-400 volumetric
infusion pump across its lifecycle, from design through production and
post-production. It applies to the risk management file RMF-VP400 and the design
FMEA FMEA-VP400.

## 2. Risk Acceptability Criteria

Risk is estimated on a 5×5 matrix of severity (1–5, where 5 is catastrophic) and
probability (1–5). The **risk acceptability threshold is a risk index of 15**: any
risk scoring 15 or above is unacceptable and requires risk control; risks scoring
below 15 are acceptable. This threshold shall be established before any risk is
evaluated against it and shall be applied consistently across the risk management
file and the design FMEA.

## 3. Risk Control Priority

Risk control measures shall be selected in the following order of priority: first,
inherently safe design and manufacture; second, protective measures in the device
or in manufacture; third, information for safety in the labeling. Where a
lower-priority control is selected, the record shall document why the higher
priority options were not practicable.

## 4. Overall Residual Risk

After all risk control measures are implemented and verified, the overall residual
risk shall be evaluated as a whole and recorded as a distinct conclusion with its
own justification. Any individual residual risk that remains above the
acceptability threshold requires a documented benefit-risk analysis before the
overall residual risk can be judged acceptable.

## 5. Production and Post-Production

The plan requires that complaint, service, and field information be reviewed
against the risk estimates, and that the risk file be updated where that
information shows a risk estimate is no longer valid.

## 6. Responsibilities

Systems Engineering maintains the risk management file and the FMEA. The Quality
Manager approves the risk management review before commercial release.
