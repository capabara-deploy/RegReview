# CAPA-2026-0188 — Intermittent display blanking, SP-200 Syringe Pump

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Record | CAPA-2026-0188 |
| Opened | 2026-03-04 |
| Closed | 2026-03-27 |
| Owner | M. Ortiz, Manufacturing Engineering |
| Approver | K. Reyes, Quality Manager |

## 1. Problem statement

Between 2026-01-12 and 2026-02-28, field service logged 41 reports of the SP-200
front-panel display blanking for two to six seconds during an active infusion.
Infusion delivery continued uninterrupted in every reported case, and no report
described a change in delivered volume. Approximately 1,900 units are in service.

## 2. Investigation

The cause was determined to be a marginal solder joint at display ribbon
connector J4.

Supply-rail ripple was evaluated and excluded: rail measurements taken from
twelve returned units were within specification across the full load range, and
are recorded in TR-2026-118 rev B.

## 3. Risk assessment

The failure mode does not affect delivered volume or occlusion detection. The
existing hazard analysis rates loss of display for under ten seconds as
acceptable without additional control, and this event falls within that
duration.

## 4. Actions taken

1. Correction — the reflow profile for connector J4 was adjusted on the SP-200
   production line, effective 2026-03-18.
2. The operator at that station was retrained on the revised profile.
3. Supplier notification regarding incoming ribbon connector tolerance.

No field action was required, as units already in service are unaffected.

## 5. Effectiveness verification

Effectiveness was verified by the absence of further service reports during the
thirty days following closure.

Because no reports were received in that window, the corrective action is
considered fully effective across the installed base.

## 6. Field disposition

Rework of the twenty-two units returned under warranty was completed on
2026-04-10 and the reworked units were returned to service.

## 7. Preventive action

Preventive action: not applicable.

## 8. Closure

The record was closed on 2026-03-27 with action item 3 still open.

Results achieved were recorded in the engineering file.
