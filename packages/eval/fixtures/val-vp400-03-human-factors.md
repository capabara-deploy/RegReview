# VP-400 Summative Human-Factors / Use Validation Report

**Document No.:** VAL-VP400-03
**Rev:** 1
**Date:** 2026-02-02
**Author:** L. Marchetti, Systems Engineering

---

## 1. Purpose

This report documents summative human-factors validation of the VP-400 against its
intended use and use environment, per the human-factors plan. It validates the
design inputs in DIR-0400 related to operator use.

## 2. Method

Fifteen representative clinical users performed the critical tasks of programming
an infusion, responding to an occlusion alarm, and removing the administration-set
cassette. Use errors and close calls were recorded and analyzed for harm potential.

## 3. Critical Tasks and Results

| Task | Result |
|---|---|
| Program infusion at target rate | All participants completed successfully |
| Respond to occlusion alarm | All participants recognized and responded |
| Remove cassette after closing roller clamp | 13 of 15 closed the clamp before removal |

## 4. Findings

Two participants removed the cassette without first closing the roller clamp,
consistent with the free-flow hazard HAZ-02. The Instructions for Use warning
(Section 4.3) and training were reviewed as the mitigations for this use error.

## 5. Conclusion

The VP-400 is validated for its intended use by trained clinical staff, with the
labeled warnings and training in place.

---

*This validation traces to the use-related design inputs in DIR-0400 and to the
free-flow hazard HAZ-02 in RMF-VP400.*
