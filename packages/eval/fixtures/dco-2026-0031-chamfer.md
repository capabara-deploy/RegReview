# Engineering Change Order — Cassette Channel Chamfer, VP-400

**Record No.:** DCO-2026-0031
**Rev:** 1
**Date:** 2026-03-12
**Originator:** K. Sethi, Design Engineering
**Governing procedure:** QSP-0015 Engineering Change Control and Change Assessment

---

## 1. Reason for Change

This change implements risk control **RC-118** and the corrective action from
CAPA-2026-0142. A chamfer is added to the administration-set cassette channel
guide to reduce the likelihood of the tubing being seated at an angle, which has
been associated with spurious "Occlusion Detected" alarms.

## 2. Affected Models

VP-400.

## 3. Affected Documents

- DOS-0400 (Design Output Specification / DMR index) — cassette channel drawing
  updated.

## 4. Description of Change

The cassette channel guide geometry is revised to add a chamfer at the tubing
entry. Tooling is modified accordingly.

## 5. Effectivity

Effective for **new production only**, beginning 2026-03-20. Units already
manufactured and distributed are not reworked.

## 6. Change Assessment

The change is a mechanical geometry revision to the disposable-set channel guide.
It does not alter the indications for use or the delivery mechanism. A separate
submission determination is recorded under the change-control process.

## 7. Disposition of Distributed Units

Not addressed in this change order.

## 8. Risk File Assessment

Not addressed in this change order.
