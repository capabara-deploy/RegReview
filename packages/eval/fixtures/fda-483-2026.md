The observations below were made during the inspection of your facility. They do
not represent a final Agency determination regarding your compliance.

## Observation 1

Corrective and preventive action activities did not verify that the corrective
action was effective. Specifically, for CAPA-2026-0142, addressing an increase in
spurious occlusion alarms on the VP-400 infusion pump, effectiveness was recorded
on the basis that a corrective change was present on sampled units and that
training was delivered, rather than on objective evidence that the observed alarm
rate had changed. The effectiveness check was performed approximately ten days
after the corrective change was implemented, and your procedure QSP-0012 requires
verification against a change in the observed failure rate over a minimum
ninety-day window.

## Observation 2

Information from complaints and post-production sources was not adequately fed back
into the risk management file. Specifically, CAPA-2026-0114 determined that the
occlusion alarm failed to annunciate in 6 of 40 units bench-tested, but the design
FMEA (FMEA-VP400 Rev 11) and the risk management file (RMF-VP400 Rev 4) were not
updated to reflect this finding and continue to carry the prior occurrence and
detection estimates for the affected failure modes.

## Observation 3

A change to a distributed device was not assessed for its effect on units already
in the field. Specifically, the cassette-channel geometry revision implemented
under DCO-2026-0031 as a corrective action for CAPA-2026-0142 was applied to new
production only, and neither the change order nor the CAPA records a disposition of
the approximately 35,000 to 41,000 units already distributed, nor an assessment of
the change against the device risk management file.

## Observation 4

Your firm did not document a determination of reportability for a complaint
involving a possible serious injury. Specifically, complaint COMP-2026-0207
described an interruption of a vasoactive infusion associated with a transient
drop in blood pressure requiring clinical intervention, and no reportability
determination under 21 CFR Part 803 and no Medical Safety Officer escalation was
recorded, contrary to your procedures QSP-0008 and QSP-0012.
