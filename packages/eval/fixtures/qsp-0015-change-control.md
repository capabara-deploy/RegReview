# Quality System Procedure — Engineering Change Control and Change Assessment

**Document No.:** QSP-0015
**Rev:** 3
**Effective Date:** 2025-06-01
**Owner:** Director of Engineering

---

1. Purpose

   This procedure defines the process for controlling engineering changes to
   released products and for assessing whether a change to a cleared device
   requires a new premarket submission.

2. Scope

   Applies to all changes to released Northlake devices, their software, their
   labeling, and their manufacturing processes.

3. References

   QSP-0021 Risk Management. QSP-0012 Corrective and Preventive Action. FDA
   guidance "Deciding When to Submit a 510(k) for a Change to an Existing Device."

4. Definitions

   ECO — Engineering Change Order. LTF — Letter-to-File. Cleared configuration —
   the device configuration described in the most recent 510(k) clearance.

5. Change Initiation

   5.1 Each change shall be recorded on an engineering change order that
       identifies the affected documents, the affected device models, and the
       reason for the change.

   5.2 Where a change affects a component shared across more than one device
       model, the change order shall identify every affected model and the change
       shall be assessed against the risk file of each.

6. Change Assessment Against the Cleared Device

   6.1 Every change to a cleared device shall be assessed for whether it requires
       a new 510(k), and the assessment shall be documented before the change is
       implemented.

   6.2 The comparison in the change assessment shall be made against the
       most-recently-cleared configuration of the device, identified by its
       510(k) number, and not against an intervening unreleased or internally
       revised configuration.

   6.3 Where changes have accumulated since the last clearance, the assessment
       shall evaluate the changes both separately and in the aggregate, and shall
       state whether their combined effect alters the basis of the prior
       clearance.

   6.4 The assessment shall record the comparison of the changed attributes to
       the cleared configuration in a documented comparison table.

7. Risk Assessment of Changes

   7.1 Every change shall be assessed against the risk management file, and the
       assessment shall confirm whether the change introduces a new hazard,
       alters an existing risk estimate, or adds or modifies a risk control.

   7.2 Where a change adds or modifies a risk control, the change shall be treated
       as significant for the purpose of the submission decision unless a
       documented rationale establishes otherwise.

8. Records

   The change order, the change assessment, and the submission determination
   shall be retained in the design file for the affected device, and shall
   identify the approver of the determination.
