# VP-400 Instructions for Use

**Document No.:** IFU-VP400
**Rev:** 7
**Date:** 2025-12-01
**Owner:** Technical Publications

---

## 1. Intended Use

The VP-400 Volumetric Infusion Pump is intended for the controlled delivery of
intravenous fluids and medications to adult and pediatric patients by trained
clinical staff in professional healthcare facilities.

## 2. Setup

Load the administration-set cassette into the pump until it seats fully. Confirm
the cassette is seated squarely before starting the infusion. Angled seating may
affect pressure sensing.

## 3. Programming

Enter the prescribed rate and volume. Confirm the entered rate before starting.
Dose-limit soft alerts, where configured by the hospital pharmacy, will warn of
entries outside the configured limits.

## 4. Alarms

### 4.1 Occlusion Alarm

If an "Occlusion Detected" alarm annunciates, check the downstream line for
kinks or closed clamps and check that the administration set is seated squarely.
Restart the infusion once the cause is resolved.

### 4.3 Removing the Cassette

**Warning:** Close the roller clamp on the administration set before removing the
cassette from the pump. Removing the cassette without closing the roller clamp can
allow gravity free-flow to the patient.

## 5. Maintenance

Refer to the service manual for periodic maintenance and calibration intervals.
