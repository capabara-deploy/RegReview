# Complaint Record — VP-400 Infusion Interruption During Vasoactive Infusion

**Record No.:** COMP-2026-0207
**Rev:** 1
**Date Received:** 2026-01-28
**Reporter:** Clinical engineering, reporting hospital (account 4471)
**Device:** VP-400 Volumetric Infusion Pump, software version 3.2.1
**Governing procedure:** QSP-0008 Complaint Handling and MDR Reportability

---

## 1. Complaint as Received

The reporting site stated that a VP-400 pump delivering a norepinephrine
(vasoactive) infusion to an adult ICU patient annunciated an "Occlusion Detected"
alarm and stopped the infusion. Clinical staff reported that the administration
set appeared correctly seated and that no downstream occlusion was found on
inspection. The infusion was interrupted for an estimated 12 minutes before the
pump was restarted on a second channel.

During the interruption the patient experienced a transient drop in blood pressure
that required the care team to intervene by increasing support. The site reported
that the patient recovered and that no permanent impairment was identified.

## 2. Device and Configuration

The pump was running application software version 3.2.1. The site returned the
pump for evaluation.

## 3. Initial Categorization

The complaint describes an interruption of a vasoactive infusion with a
clinical intervention. The event is categorized as an infusion-interruption
complaint and forwarded to Field Quality for investigation.

## 4. Investigation Reference

This complaint is one of a group of "Occlusion Detected" alarm complaints on the
VP-400 opened for systemic review under CAPA-2026-0142.

## 5. Status

Open — forwarded to CAPA-2026-0142 for investigation and disposition.
