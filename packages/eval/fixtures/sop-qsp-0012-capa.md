# Quality System Procedure — Corrective and Preventive Action

**Document No.:** QSP-0012
**Rev:** 6
**Effective Date:** 2025-09-01
**Owner:** Director of Quality Assurance

---

1. Purpose

   This procedure defines the process for identifying, investigating, and
   resolving nonconformities and potential nonconformities.

2. Scope

   Applies to all product lines manufactured or distributed by the organization.

3. References

   QSP-0008 Complaint Handling. QSP-0021 Risk Management. ISO 13485:2016.

4. Definitions

   CAPA — Corrective and Preventive Action. NC — Nonconformity.

5. Initiation and Risk Screening

   5.1 The originator shall record the problem statement, including the
       quantity affected, the date range of occurrence, and the product
       identifiers involved.

   5.2 The originator shall complete an initial risk screening within three
       business days of opening the record, and shall document whether the
       event involves actual or potential patient harm.

   5.3 Where the event involves actual or potential patient harm, the record
       shall be escalated to the Medical Safety Officer for a reportability
       determination, and the determination and its basis shall be recorded in
       the CAPA record itself.

6. Investigation

   6.1 The investigation team shall identify the root cause using a structured
       method, and shall name the method used in the record.

   6.2 The record shall document why each candidate cause that was considered
       and eliminated was ruled out, including the evidence relied upon.

   6.3 Where the affected population is bounded by a product configuration,
       software version, or lot range, the investigation shall explain why that
       boundary is the correct one.

7. Corrective Action and Change Assessment

   7.1 Each action shall name a responsible owner and a target completion date.

   7.2 Where an action changes a product, process, or specification, the record
       shall reference the change control record and shall confirm the change
       was assessed against the risk management file.

   7.3 Where a design or hardware change is applied to new production only, the
       record shall document the disposition of units already distributed.

8. Effectiveness Verification

   8.1 The effectiveness acceptance criterion shall be defined and approved
       before the corrective action is implemented.

   8.2 Effectiveness shall be verified against objective evidence of a change in
       the observed failure rate, measured over a minimum observation window of
       ninety days following implementation. Confirmation that an action was
       completed shall not by itself constitute effectiveness verification.

   8.3 The verification sample size shall be justified with respect to the
       failure rate the action is intended to reduce.

9. Closure

   9.1 The record shall not be closed until every action item has a documented
       outcome and the effectiveness verification is complete and approved.

   9.2 Where ongoing monitoring is required after closure, the record shall
       identify the successor record or monitoring activity by document number.

   9.3 Closure shall be approved by the Quality Manager, and the approval date
       shall not precede the date of the evidence relied upon.

10. Records

    All records generated under this procedure shall be retained per QSP-0030
    and shall be filed under the record type "CAPA Record", not as meeting
    minutes or general correspondence.
